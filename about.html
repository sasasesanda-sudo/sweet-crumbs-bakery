About Us

History:

Founded in 2018 as a small kitchen project, Sweet Crumbs Bakery has grown into a community favourite.

Mission:

To bring joy to our community by crafting delicious, fresh and affordable baked goods every day.

Vision Statement:

To become the region's most loved sustainable bakery, known for quality and heart.
